Skarven

Eleven rooms on a ridge above the Skarven sound, open from May to October.

Inside the cloud, 800 metres up

Below the cloud, the whole sound opens.

Eleven rooms and one long table

Skarven sits at 1,140 metres on the north ridge. Most mornings the cloud fills the sound and burns off by nine; from then on you can count the boats. The last stretch is a stone path, forty minutes on foot, and the goods lift carries your bags.
