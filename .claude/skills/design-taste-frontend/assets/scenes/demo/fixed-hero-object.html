Small enough to always take

A fixed 35 mm lens, a quiet leaf shutter and a body cut from one block of aluminium. Nothing to swap.

A shutter you barely hear

The leaf shutter sits inside the lens, so nothing slaps or swings when you press. At a reading, a market or a sleeping child, the loudest thing in the room is still the room.
