Skarfjell Observatory

A public observatory 1,420 metres up the Skarfjell ridge, an hour above the valley by the last cable car, open to guests on clear winter nights.

How a night on the ridge runs

  1. 17:40

    The last cable car leaves Hallsdal. Wear boots: the path from the top station is ten minutes on packed snow, lit at ankle height.

  2. 19:00

    Soup and rye bread in the hut while the domes cool to the night air, and a short talk on what is up tonight.

  3. 22:00

    Every light on the ridge goes out. The east dome opens on whatever stands highest; the west dome is yours to steer.

  4. 01:30

    The night car takes you down. If the sky closes in, we text you by four in the afternoon and move you to the next clear night.

Book a clear night

Nights run from October to March, Thursday to Saturday, for up to 24 guests. A night costs 640 kr with the cable car both ways, soup in the hut and two hours under the domes.

We watch the forecast for you. If the sky is closed, your night moves to the next clear one, or you get the money back.
