Aldern makes films for walls.

A studio of three in Rotterdam. We shoot, grade and install moving pictures cut for one surface and the people who pass it.

Swim, a film for one wall

Twelve minutes of cold water, filmed at first light on Lake Varda.

Swim, 2026. Nightly under the Varda viaduct.

One wall at a time.

Every film we make is cut for the surface it plays on: its size, its light, its distance from the pavement. Swim played under the viaduct all summer.
