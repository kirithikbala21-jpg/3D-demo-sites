Varn High Route

Nine days on foot across the Varn glaciers, from Ossen to Valtar, sleeping every night in a staffed hut. Open from 1 July to 14 September.

See the stages

Above the tree line

Seven cols and two glaciers between Ossen and Valtar, all of it on foot.

Where the sky begins

Long days on the snow, warm rooms at night.

Nine huts

One hut a night

Each hut sleeps thirty to sixty in shared rooms and serves dinner, breakfast and a packed lunch. Book the route once and we hold your bed in all nine, on the nights you choose.

On the two glacier days a guide walks with groups of eight. The rest of the path is marked, and every hut keeper knows you are coming.
