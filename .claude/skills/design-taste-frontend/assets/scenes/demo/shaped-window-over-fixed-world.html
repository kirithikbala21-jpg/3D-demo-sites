Sorrel Pass Huts

Five timber huts a day's walk apart on the high route over the Sorrel icefield, open from late June until the first snow.

Icefield

Day three is the icefield itself: seven hours on old snow, roped to a guide, between two huts that each sleep twelve and keep the stove lit from six.

See the route

A week above the treeline

Twelve beds in every hut, and a stove that is lit from six.

The route opens on 24 June

Beds are booked hut by hut, in order along the route, from 2 March. Write to the warden with your dates and the size of your party, and she will hold the beds for a week while you arrange the rest.

Blankets, supper and breakfast are in the price. Bring a sleeping sheet, crampons and a head torch.
