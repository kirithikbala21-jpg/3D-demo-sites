Stone stairs, cut by hand

Flint is a workshop of six masons in Porto building staircases, steps and thresholds from Portuguese limestone and granite, eight commissions a year.

Every stair is drawn on site and cut in our yard

We measure the room, draw each tread at full size and cut it from a single block. A staircase takes six to fourteen weeks from the first visit to the last stone, and it is fitted by the people who cut it.

Commissions for spring 2027 are open. Tell us about the room and we will come and measure it.
