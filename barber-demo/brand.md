## Reviews
There are **no real Google reviews** in this config, so none are quoted here. Any customer quotes on the site are invented sample content and are labelled as sample. When the owner has real reviews they go into the site's reviews data **word for word**, with first name and date, never edited and never mixed with sample ones.

## Tone of voice
Confident, dry and friendly. Short lines, a little humour, no macho clichés. Australian spelling. Say what things cost. Never claim "best", "number one" or exact results. Everything stays clearly a concept demo.

## Style notes (mood and layout ideas only; nothing copied from the references)
1. Mood: dark, moody and classic; monochrome street or chair photography with a single metallic gold or brass accent (melbournebarbershop.com).
2. Hero: full-bleed black-and-white photo with a centred wordmark and thin spaced capitals; the headline carries the suburb.
3. Booking button: Book is the one filled button in the top bar and repeats as one big button in the hero; on phones it sits in the sticky bar (Little Rebel links Book Now straight to its booking page).
4. Reviews: quotes with the reviewer name and a rating summary with the review count (Scissors & Scotch), shown only when real.
5. Colours: near-black, warm off-white and brass gold; blush pink is Little Rebel's own accent and is not used here.
