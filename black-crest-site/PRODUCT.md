# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users
Owners of small local businesses in Melbourne: barbers, cafes, gyms, salons and trades (plumbers and similar). They run the shop themselves, are busy, and look at the site on a phone between customers. Many have no website, or an old one that loses them customers. They arrive in several ways (in person with a QR flyer, social media, Google search); no one route dominates yet (owner, Nov 2026).

## Product Purpose
KB Web Designs (formerly Black Crest Web Designs) designs and builds websites for these businesses. Success is an owner asking for the free concept homepage, then going ahead with a build.

## Positioning
The owner sees their own homepage, designed for free, before paying anything. Fixed, published prices. The owner owns the finished site, the domain and the logins; no lock-in contract.

## Operating Context
- Offer, in order: a free concept homepage (usually within 5 business days of the first chat); if the owner goes ahead, a 50% deposit starts the build; build takes 1 to 2 weeks; 2 rounds of changes; launch 1 to 2 days after the preview is approved; balance due at launch.
- Replies within 1 business day. Contact: kb.ventures@outlook.com and the contact and start forms (Web3Forms).
- Printable QR code page (qr.html) opens the free concept page; a 1-minute quiz checks an existing site; trade pages (barbers, cafes, gyms, salons, tradies) and three short guides.
- A KB Ventures company. Based in Melbourne.

## Capabilities and Constraints
- Prices (never change without the owner): One Page $300, Full Website (up to about 5 pages) $700, Care plan $30 a month (optional), Custom by quote. Domain about $20 a year, paid by the owner, in their name. Hosting setup included.
- Static HTML, CSS and JS built by `tools/build.py`, served on GitHub Pages. No cookies, tracking or analytics.
- The forms, legal pages (privacy, terms), contact details and prices stay as they are during the redesign.

## Brand Commitments
- Name: KB Web Designs, formerly Black Crest Web Designs (rebranded 9 Nov 2026; live at web.kbventures.com.au).
- Keep: the KB shield logo (the fine-line shield with KB letters; may be gilded in the new style), gold as the brand colour, and the black brushed-titanium member's card as the hero object, with its video (owner, 9 Nov 2026).
- Everything else about the look may change (owner chose a new look per brand, 9 Nov 2026).
- Australian spelling, short plain sentences. No em dashes.

## Evidence on Hand
- Six concept sites for made-up businesses, each with a case study: Brass & Blade (barber), Ember & Oak (cafe), Iron & Ember (gym), Waverley Plumbing (plumber), Maison Lune (salon), Torque & Co. (parts store). Screenshots in `assets/gallery/` and `img/work/`; the live demos are in the repo's demo folders.
- The card render, its hero video and poster (`assets/renders/`, `assets/video/`), a before/after comparison image (`img/compare-*`), a reel (`assets/reel.*`).
- No paid client work yet, no testimonials, reviews, client logos or statistics. Never invent them. Never show, link or mention the-one-barber-demo.

## Product Principles
1. Show, then ask: the free concept and the concept sites do the persuading, not claims.
2. Prices and terms stated plainly, once, where the owner needs them.
3. Built for a busy owner on a phone: fast, short, one clear next step.
4. Nothing invented: every number is a real price, commitment or measurement.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
WCAG AA contrast, full keyboard use, visible focus, reduced-motion support, alt text everywhere.
