About

Quality websites, without the agency price.

Black Crest Web Designs makes fast, good-looking websites for local businesses in Melbourne: the barbers, cafes, gyms, salons and tradies that keep a suburb going.

What we stand for

Three things we do not compromise on.

Plain English

No jargon and no hard sell. You always know what you are paying for and why.

Well made

Fast, secure, easy to use on a phone, and built to last. We sweat the details so your customers do not notice them.

Yours

You own the finished site and your domain. No lock-in contracts, ever.

How we work

See it first. Decide after.

We design your homepage for free before you pay anything. If you like it, a 50% deposit starts the build and the balance is due at launch. Two rounds of changes are included.

Our promises

What you can hold us to.

  • A free concept homepage before you pay anything.
  • Clear prices, set before we start. No hidden costs.
  • A reply within 24 hours.
  • No lock-in contracts.
  • You own your site and your domain.
  • No fake reviews or made-up numbers. We show concept sites and will earn real reviews.

A KB Ventures company

Part of something bigger.

Black Crest Web Designs is a brand of KB Ventures, a Melbourne company that starts and runs small businesses to one standard: clear, fair and well made.
