Care plan

We look after it. You run your business.

Optional, month to month, and you can cancel any time.

Care planOptional

$30 a month

What it covers

  • Hosting upkeep. We keep your site online and keep its hosting settings and HTTPS certificate up to date.
  • Small changes. Up to 3 small text or photo changes a month, like new prices, new hours or swapping a photo.
  • Security checks. We check your site and its settings each month and fix anything that needs it.
  • Backups. We keep a copy of your site, so it can be put back quickly if anything goes wrong.
  • Priority replies. Care plan requests go to the front of the queue.

What it does not cover

  • New pages or new sections (we quote these first)
  • Redesigns or a new look
  • Writing large amounts of new content from scratch
  • Your domain name renewal, which you pay directly (about $20 a year)
  • Other services you sign up to, like booking apps or email accounts
  • Unused changes do not roll over to the next month

Not on the care plan? You can still ask for changes at any time. We quote them fairly first.
