Websites for cafes.

Your menu, hours and location: easy to find, quick to load. We work with cafes across Melbourne and its suburbs.

Get a free conceptSee pricing

Why it matters

Why cafes need a good site.

  1. 01

    People check before they come

    Before a visit, people look up your hours and menu. If they cannot find them, they often go somewhere else.

  2. 02

    A menu that stays current

    A menu on your own site is easy to update and easy to read on a phone. No blurry photo of a board.

  3. 03

    Works with Google

    Your website and your free Google Business Profile work together, so the right details show in Search and Maps.

What you usually need

The features that matter.

  • Menu that is easy to update
  • Opening hours, including public holidays
  • Map and directions
  • Link to online ordering, if you use it
  • Photos of your food and space

Every site also comes with writing help, a contact form, Google basics and HTTPS.
