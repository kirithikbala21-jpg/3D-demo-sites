Why it matters

Why gyms need a good site.

  1. 01

    Answer the first questions

    New members want the classes, the times and the cost. A good site answers all three before they ask.

  2. 02

    Make trying easy

    A simple free-trial or visit form turns interest into a first session.

  3. 03

    Show the space

    Photos of your floor, equipment and coaches help people picture themselves training with you.

What you usually need

The features that matter.

  • Class timetable
  • Membership options and prices
  • Free-trial or visit form
  • Coach profiles
  • Location, parking and hours

Every site also comes with writing help, a contact form, Google basics and HTTPS.
