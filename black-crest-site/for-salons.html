Why it matters

Why salons need a good site.

  1. 01

    Clients want to see prices

    A clear service menu with prices helps clients choose and cuts down on questions.

  2. 02

    Booking at any hour

    A booking button linked to your booking app lets clients book at night, while you are closed.

  3. 03

    Your work sells itself

    A gallery of your work and your stylists shows clients what to expect before they walk in.

What you usually need

The features that matter.

  • Service menu with prices
  • Booking button for your booking app
  • Gallery of your work
  • Stylist profiles
  • Gift vouchers, if you sell them
