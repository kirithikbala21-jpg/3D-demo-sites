06/Process

Four steps. No surprises.

You see your homepage before you pay anything. This is everything that happens after that.

The full process
  1. 01
    Usually the same or next day

    Chat

    Tell us what you do, where you are and what people should do on your site: call, book or visit. A short message is enough. Send links to your logo, photos or old site if you have them.

  2. 02
    Usually within 5 business days

    Free concept

    We design your homepage around your name, your trade and your suburb. It costs nothing and commits you to nothing. Not for you? Walk away and owe nothing.

  3. 03
    Usually 1 to 2 weeks after the deposit

    Build

    Like it? We email a quote and the payment details, and a 50% deposit starts the build. We write the words with you, add your photos, the contact form and the Google basics, then share a private preview. Two rounds of changes are included.

  4. 04
    Usually 1 to 2 days after you approve it

    Launch

    You pay the balance. We connect your domain, switch on HTTPS and check every page on phones and computers. Then we hand it over: the site, the domain and the logins are yours.

11/FAQ

Straight answers.

The questions everyone asks before they start.

How much does a website cost?

A One Page site is $300. A Full Website, up to about 5 pages, is $700. The care plan is optional at $30 a month, and bigger projects are quoted. The only other cost is your own domain name, about $20 a year. See pricing.

How long does it take?

Your free concept homepage is usually ready within 5 business days of our first chat. The build usually takes 1 to 2 weeks after the deposit, and launch takes 1 to 2 days once you approve the preview.

Who owns the website?

You do. Once the balance is paid, the finished site is yours. Your domain name is registered in your name, so it is always yours too.

Can I ask for changes?

Yes. Two rounds of changes are included while we build. After launch, small text and photo changes are part of the care plan. Anything bigger is quoted fairly before we start it.

What about the domain name and hosting?

You pay for your own domain name (your web address), about $20 a year, registered in your name. Hosting setup is included in the price. If anything about hosting would cost you extra, we tell you before you say yes.
