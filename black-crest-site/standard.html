01/The Black Crest Standard

Ten promises, in writing.

These hold for every project, whatever the size. If we ever fall short of one, tell us at kb.ventures@outlook.com.

  1. 01

    A free concept, first

    We design your homepage before you pay anything. If it is not right for you, you walk away and owe nothing.

  2. 02

    One price, in writing

    Every price is agreed in writing before work starts. One Page is $300, a Full Website is $700, bigger projects are quoted.

  3. 03

    Half now, half at launch

    A 50% deposit starts the build. The balance is due when your site goes live, never before.

  4. 04

    Two rounds of changes

    Two rounds of changes are included in every build, so the site ends up the way you want it.

  5. 05

    You own it

    Once the balance is paid, the finished site is yours: the files, the logins and the right to take it anywhere.

  6. 06

    Your domain, in your name

    Your web address is registered in your name and paid for by you, about $20 a year. Nobody can hold it back from you.

  7. 07

    No lock-in

    The care plan is $30 a month, month to month. Cancel by email before the next month starts.

  8. 08

    Built for phones

    Every page is designed for phones first and checked on phones and computers before launch.

  9. 09

    No tracking we did not discuss

    We do not add analytics, cookies or trackers to your site unless you ask for them. This site has none.

  10. 10

    Honest words only

    No made-up reviews, invented numbers or borrowed logos, on your site or on ours.
