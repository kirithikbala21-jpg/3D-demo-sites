      Our story

      Started with a second-hand roaster and a very patient landlord.

      Ember & Oak began in 2016 in a converted timber workshop, where a tiny drum roaster sat between the sink and the stockroom. We roasted at night, tasted at dawn and gave away more cups than we sold.

      Today the roaster still lives out back, and the oak tables are still scratched. You are welcome to stay until the second coffee goes cold.
