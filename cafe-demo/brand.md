## Reviews
There are **no real Google reviews** in this config, so none are quoted here. Any customer quotes on the site are invented sample content and are labelled as sample. When the owner has real reviews they go into the site's reviews data **word for word**, with first name and date, never edited and never mixed with sample ones.

## Tone of voice
Warm, relaxed, a bit playful. Talks about coffee, food and the room, never about being "the best". Australian spelling. Say what things cost. Never claim "best", "number one" or exact results. Everything stays clearly a concept demo.

## Style notes (mood and layout ideas only; nothing copied from the references)
1. Mood: warm, cosy and image-led (Hygge) with a bold single-photo opening (Culture Espresso).
2. Hero: full-bleed photo, huge bold headline, minimal navigation; centred branding with two clear links beneath.
3. Booking/order button: one big Order (pick-up) or Reserve button under the headline; further buttons sit directly under each section's text (Colectivo uses "Shop/See" buttons under each tile).
4. Reviews: short quotes shown on product or menu cards; Google rating only when real.
5. Colours: espresso brown, cream and one ember/terracotta accent, with warm wood tones.
