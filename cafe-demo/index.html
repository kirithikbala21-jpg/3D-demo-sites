Our story

Started with a second-hand roaster and a very patient landlord.

Ember & Oak began in 2016 in a converted timber workshop, where a tiny drum roaster sat between the sink and the stockroom. We roasted at night, tasted at dawn and gave away more cups than we sold. The neighbours didn’t seem to mind.

Today the roaster still lives out back. We buy from a handful of farms we know by name, roast in small batches every week, and bake everything on the premises. The room is warm, the oak tables are scratched, and you’re welcome to stay until the second coffee goes cold.

From farm to cup

  1. 01

    Source

    We visit or call every producer, pay above fair-trade floor prices and buy by the lot, not the sack.

  2. 02

    Roast

    Small, slow batches on a 15kg drum, cupped the next morning and sold within ten days.

  3. 03

    Brew

    Water, grind and time dialled in every day, because humidity and weather change the shot.

  4. 04

    Pour

    Served in warm ceramic, with compostable cups and a ten-cent discount if you bring your own.

Good to know

Questions, answered.

Still stuck? Call us on 03 5550 0123 and a human will pick up.

Do I need to book?

Walk-ins are always welcome. We recommend booking for weekend brunch, evenings and any group of 6 or more.

Can you cater for allergies and dietary needs?

Yes. Most of the menu can be made vegetarian, vegan, gluten free or dairy free. Tell us when you book, and we’ll talk you through it on the day. We do handle nuts and gluten in our kitchen.

Is there a minimum spend for private dining?

The oak room has a minimum spend that varies by day and season. Send an enquiry and we’ll reply within one business day.

Do you do takeaway and online orders?

Yes. Order at the counter, and our beans are available to take home any time. A pre-order service is coming soon (in this fictional world).

Can I bring my own cup?

Please do. You’ll get ten cents off every drink, and our compostable cups are one less thing in landfill.

Do gift cards expire?

Never within three years, and they can be used for anything in the café, including events and private dining.
