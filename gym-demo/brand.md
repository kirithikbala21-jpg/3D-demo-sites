## Reviews
There are **no real Google reviews** in this config, so none are quoted here. Any customer quotes on the site are invented sample content and are labelled as sample. When the owner has real reviews they go into the site's reviews data **word for word**, with first name and date, never edited and never mixed with sample ones.

## Tone of voice
Direct, encouraging, no shaming. Short punchy lines, honest about what a class is like. Australian spelling. Say what things cost. Never claim "best", "number one" or exact results. Everything stays clearly a concept demo.

## Style notes (mood and layout ideas only; nothing copied from the references)
1. Mood: dark, energetic and community-first (alchemy365.com, lumberjack-athletics.com, tribebx.co).
2. Hero: full-bleed action photo under a dark gradient on the left, a small uppercase eyebrow with the place name, a big uppercase headline, then ONE bright pill button.
3. Booking button: "Book a class" / free-trial button in the hero (Tribe stacks a Book and a Trial button; Alchemy leads with one trial offer), repeated in the sticky bar on phones.
4. Reviews: member quotes with first names and a Google rating summary, only when real.
5. Colours: black or near-black plus a single vivid accent (blue, red or magenta in the references).
