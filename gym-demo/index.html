    The offer

    7 days free. No lock-in.

    Train for a full week at Iron & Ember Athletic Club before you decide. Open gym, classes and a coach to show you around, with no card and no contract.

    Start your free trial
    What’s included

    Seven days of open gym and any class with a spare spot, a 45 minute coached session on day one, a tour of the floor and the recovery room, and a locker for the week.

    What happens next

    Book your first session, turn up in training clothes and meet your coach. At the end of the week we ask if you want to join. If not, you walk away with nothing owing and no follow-up calls.

    Costs

    The trial is $0. We do not ask for a card. If you join, plans start at $79 a month on annual billing, or $89 month to month with no lock-in and 14 days notice to cancel.

    Meet the coaches

    Four coaches. One standard.

    • Marcus Quinn

      Strength · Powerlifting basics · Return to training

      Marcus opened Iron & Ember in 2012 after a decade coaching in other people’s gyms. He runs the strength programs and still coaches the 5:30am crowd.

      Leads: Barbell Basics, Hips & Shoulders, Lift Lab

      Book with Marcus →

    • Jonah Reyes

      HIIT · Boxing · Fat loss

      A former amateur boxer who turned his footwork into a coaching career. Jonah runs the HIIT and boxing classes with a loud playlist and a quiet eye for technique.

      Leads: Boxing Conditioning, Boxing Fundamentals, Ember HIIT, Sprint Intervals

      Book with Jonah →

    • Dev Nair

      Yoga · Mobility · Recovery

      Dev teaches yoga and mobility for people who lift, run and sit at desks. Expect clear cues, honest modifications and a very long exhale.

      Leads: Mobility Reset, Slow Yoga, Stretch Club, Vinyasa Flow, Yin & Restore

      Book with Dev →

    • Dex Holloway

      Olympic lifting · Strength · Conditioning

      Dex coaches the heavy lifts and the engine-room conditioning. He loves a good deadlift, a better warm-up and explaining why your knees do that.

      Leads: Engine Room, Power Hour

      Book with Dex →
