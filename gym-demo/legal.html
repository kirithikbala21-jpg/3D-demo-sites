    Privacy

    This demo does not collect personal information. Forms (bookings, visit requests, newsletter, careers, vouchers) only check your input in your browser and then show a confirmation. Nothing is sent or stored on a server.

    If this site were live, we would collect only what we need to take a booking (name, email, phone, health notes you choose to share), use it for that purpose, keep it no longer than necessary and never sell it. You could ask us to see or delete your information at any time by emailing hello@example.com.

    Terms of use

    Iron & Ember Athletic Club is a fictional business created as a design demonstration. Names, prices, reviews, people and events on this site are invented. Nothing here is an offer to sell goods or services.

    Cookies and storage

    This demo sets no tracking cookies. It uses your browser’s local storage only to remember small preferences: your cookie choice, whether you dismissed the announcement bar, and any demo bookings or visit requests you make. If analytics were added, they would run only after you accept.
