    Good to know

    Questions, answered.

    Still stuck? Call us on 07 5550 0188 and a human will pick up.

    Do I need to book a class?

    Yes, classes have limited spots so please book ahead. Open gym needs no booking. Members can book from 7 days out, and trial guests from the trial page.

    I have never trained. Where do I start?

    Start with the free trial session. A coach walks you through the floor, learns your goals and runs a 45 minute workout at your level. Barbell Basics and Slow Yoga are friendly first classes.

    What should I bring?

    Water, a towel and shoes you can lift in. Boxing wraps are provided. Mats, bars, plates, kettlebells and bands are all here.

    Is there parking?

    There is metered parking on nearby streets, and public car parks within a five minute walk. The Valley is also well served by buses and trains.

    Do you have a recovery area?

    Yes. The recovery room has a sauna, a cold plunge and a mobility zone with rollers and bands. It is free for All Access and Coached members and $12 a visit for others.

    Is the gym accessible?

    Step-free entry, wide aisles, an accessible change room and a lift to the mezzanine. Tell us what you need and we will help.

    What if I need to cancel a class?

    Please cancel at least 4 hours before so someone on the waitlist can take your spot. Late cancellations count as an attended class.

    Is this a real gym?

    No. Iron & Ember Athletic Club is a fictional concept demo. The names, prices, quote and numbers are all invented.
