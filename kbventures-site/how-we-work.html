How we work.

Every KB Ventures business is started and run the same way.

  1. 01

    Idea

    We start with a real need and check it makes sense. Before anything is built, we look at who it is for and what they actually need. If the idea does not hold up, we do not go ahead.

  2. 02

    Build

    We build it carefully, with its own website and clear prices. Each business gets its own name, its own website and prices that are easy to understand. We take the time to get the details right before anything opens.

  3. 03

    Launch

    We open it up, check it works and fix problems fast. When a business opens, we test it ourselves and listen to the first people who use it. Anything that goes wrong is fixed as quickly as we can.

  4. 04

    Run

    We keep it running, with support and fair dealing. After launch, we keep looking after it and answer questions. If something goes wrong with a purchase, we put it right.

Our standard.

Clear

Plain words and honest prices.

We say what you get and what it costs in plain words, so there are no surprises.

Fair

Straight dealing, and refunds when something is wrong.

We deal with people the way we would want to be dealt with. If something you bought is faulty or not as described, we fix it or refund you.

Well made

Products and sites built carefully.

We take care over the details and check our work before it goes out, then keep improving it.
