Refunds policy

Last updated: 28 October 2026

In plain English

  • Study guides are digital and arrive straight after you pay.
  • If a file is faulty, won’t open, or isn’t what the product page described, we’ll fix it or refund you in full.
  • We reply within 3 business days.
  • No refunds for change of mind once downloaded, but your Australian Consumer Law rights always apply.

1. How delivery works

Our study guides are digital products. They are delivered to you immediately after payment.

2. When we fix it or refund you

Email us if your file:

  • is faulty;
  • won’t open; or
  • isn’t what the product page described.

We will fix the problem or give you a full refund. We reply within 3 business days.

3. Change of mind

Because guides are digital, we don’t give refunds for change of mind once a guide has been downloaded.

4. Your rights under the Australian Consumer Law

Nothing in this policy limits your rights under the Australian Consumer Law.

5. How to ask

Email kb.ventures@outlook.com with:

  • the email address you used for the order;
  • the name of the product; and
  • a short note on what’s wrong.

We reply within 3 business days.

6. Website services

This policy covers AceGuides digital products. For KB Websites work, the scope and price are agreed in writing before work starts; see our terms.

Contact

Problem with a purchase? Email kb.ventures@outlook.com.
