01  /  Why tint

Less heat.
Less glare.

Heat

Film blocks a share of the sun's heat, so the cabin warms up slower when the car is parked.

Glare

Low sun and headlights are softer through tinted glass.

Privacy

Bags and tools on the back seat are harder to see from the street.
