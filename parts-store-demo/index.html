          Ask a mechanic

          Real mechanics answer in one business day.

          • Can I mix oil brands?

            Yes, if the grade and spec match. Top up with the same grade when you can.

          • How often should I change brake pads?

            Most last 40,000 to 70,000 km. Squealing or a longer stop means check them now.

          • Why does my battery go flat?

            Short trips and age are common. A load test at the counter takes two minutes.
