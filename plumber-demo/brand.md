## Tone of voice
Plain, friendly and reassuring. Says what happens next, gives prices, never scares. Local and practical. Australian spelling. Say what things cost. Never claim "best", "number one" or exact results. Everything stays clearly a concept demo.

## Style notes (mood and layout ideas only; nothing copied from the references)
1. Mood: friendly, trustworthy and local; light and airy (Wilson Plumbing) or bold navy and red with a strong emergency line (Silver Water Plumbing).
2. Hero: headline with the suburb, a real team or van photo, a big phone number and a main button; Silver Water adds a booking card with stars and trust ticks beside the photo.
3. Booking button: the phone number and a "Get a quote" button sit side by side in the header and hero; a sticky top or bottom bar keeps Call and Book in view.
4. Reviews: star rating with the review count near the hero, plus quotes lower down, only when real.
5. Colours: one deep colour (navy), white or cream, and one action colour (red, blue or gold); the emergency line is the loudest element.
