Burst pipe or leak: call us now

Call 03 5550 0142

While you wait

  • Turn off the water at the mains (usually near the water meter or front tap).
  • Switch off the power at the switchboard if water is near outlets or lights.
  • Open the taps to drain the pipes, and move valuables out of the way.

No hot water: call us now

Call 03 5550 0142

While you wait

  • Check the switchboard (electric) or pilot light (gas) is still on.
  • If you smell gas, leave the house and call us from outside. Do not use switches.
  • Do not open the relief valve or touch a leaking unit while it is hot.

Blocked drain: call us now

Call 03 5550 0142

While you wait

  • Stop using sinks, showers and the toilet so the water does not rise.
  • Skip chemical drain cleaners. They can damage pipes and hurt our crew.
  • If it is overflowing, keep kids and pets away from the water.

Questions

Straight answers

Do you charge a call-out fee?

We quote a fixed price before work starts, and it includes getting to you. Emergency call-outs start from $199 and include the first 30 minutes of work. We tell you the call-out fee before the plumber leaves the depot.

Are you licensed and insured?

Yes. Every plumber and gas fitter on our team is licensed (Lic. 000000) and we carry public liability insurance. Our licence number is on every quote and invoice.

Do you work weekends?

Yes. We answer the phones from 8am to 5pm on Saturday and Sunday, and the emergency line is open 24/7, including public holidays.

Do you do gas?

Yes. We do gas fitting, appliance installs and gas leak checks from $169. If you smell gas, leave the house, do not touch any switches and call us on 03 5550 0142. In an emergency call 000.

How fast can you come?

In our service area, a plumber is usually at your door within 60 minutes. Closer suburbs are often 30 to 45 minutes. Check your suburb in the service areas section.

Do you give a warranty?

Yes. All our work carries a 12-month workmanship guarantee. Parts and hot water systems also carry the manufacturer's warranty, which is often longer.

Do you work with insurers?

Yes. We can supply photos, a written report and an itemised invoice for your insurance claim. It is worth checking with your insurer first about any approved providers.

Do you take card?

Yes. Pay by card on the day. Afterpay is available on big jobs, and businesses can pay by invoice.
