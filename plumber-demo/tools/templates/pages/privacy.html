This is a concept demo for a fictional business. {{brand.demoNotice}}

What this demo stores

The quote form does not send anything. If you submit it, your details are saved in this browser only (local storage key wp-quotes) so the demo can show you a confirmation. Photos you pick are previewed on your device and never uploaded.

The same goes for the optional "Remind me" choice (email or text) and the "Call me back" form: in this demo they are kept in this browser only (local storage keys wp-quotes and wp-callbacks). No reminder is sent and nobody is called back. {{#if formsLive}}Once the business connects its form service, the details you enter are sent to the office to arrange the reminder or call.{{else}}A real business must connect an email or text service before reminders can work, and should add it to this policy.{{/if}} The Google review button and the QR code only open the business's Google review page when you choose to use them.

Cookies and local storage

The site remembers three small choices in your browser: whether you closed the announcement bar, your cookie choice, and any quote or call-back request you submitted in this demo. It loads no advertising or tracking scripts.

Third parties

The map near the bottom of the home page is a Google Maps embed. It loads only when you scroll to it, and Google handles any data it collects under its own policy.
