# Photo credits: to fill in

Photos are placed (all from Unsplash, listed in the footer as a concept demo) but the individual photographer names and licences are not recorded yet: look each up from the original file in `img/source/` before launch. When a photo is added with `tools/add-photo.py`, pass the photographer in `--credit` (for example `"Jane Doe / Unsplash"`). It is saved in `data/photos.json` and listed in the site footer after `python3 tools/build.py`. Do not guess names: look each photo up and fill in the real photographer and licence.
