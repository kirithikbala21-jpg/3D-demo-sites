## Reviews
There are **no real Google reviews** in this config, so none are quoted here. Any customer quotes on the site are invented sample content and are labelled as sample. When the owner has real reviews they go into the site's reviews data **word for word**, with first name and date, never edited and never mixed with sample ones.

## Tone of voice
Calm, warm and plain-spoken. Short sentences, no hype, no jargon. Talks about care, listening and clear prices. Australian spelling. Say what things cost. Never claim "best", "number one" or exact results. Everything stays clearly a concept demo.

## Style notes (mood and layout ideas only; nothing copied from the references)
1. Mood: airy, editorial and calm; lots of white space, thin spaced capitals and large unhurried photos (thphair.com). Luxe Concept and Lavish were not readable from here, so no notes are taken from them.
2. Hero: one large real photo with the headline and suburb sitting on a soft shade only behind the text; the right of the photo stays bright and warm.
3. Booking button: a small "Book now" always visible in the header, plus one big Book button in the hero and the sticky Call/Book bar on phones.
4. Reviews: short quotes with a first name and the service; stars and the real Google count only when the owner has filled them in.
5. Colours: ivory, cocoa brown and a muted clay accent (sage as a quiet second accent).
