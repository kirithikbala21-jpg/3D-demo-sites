# Changes (The One Barber concept demo)

Copied from `barber-demo` (which was **not** changed) and re-made for The One Barber.

## What was removed
Every name from the original sample barbershop, every photo, barber name, price, product, shop/cart/search/sound feature and invented review.

## What was added
- English / 中文 switch (remembered in the browser)
- "Barber in Glen Waverley" section with directions and hours
- "Walk-ins welcome, open 6 days" strip (payments, kids and parking lines are from Google, owner to confirm)
- Google 5.0 / 30 reviews badge linking to Google
- Pensioner and kids prices, and a Cut + Beard combo offer (all `[price]` placeholders)
- "Meet your barber" card with "Book with him" button (name placeholder)
- Manage my booking (change or cancel, demo only) and a booking policy section (placeholder text)
- QR code to book from a phone (with print view)
- "Inside the shop" section with empty photo slots
- Instagram and Facebook links, hidden while the config value is blank
- Kept: reminders, waitlist, "usually quieter" line, open-hours banner, Google review page with QR
