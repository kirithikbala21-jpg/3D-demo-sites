Privacy

{{#if formsLive}}When you send a booking or waitlist request, the details you type (name, phone, what you asked for) are sent to the shop so it can reply and run the appointment. They are not sold or shared.{{else}}This demo does not collect personal information. The booking, waitlist and reminder forms only check your input in your browser and then show a confirmation. Nothing is sent to the shop or stored on a server.{{/if}} No analytics or advertising scripts are loaded unless you accept them.

Reminders are optional. If you choose a reminder (email or text) the contact detail is used only to send that reminder. In this demo sending is not connected, so nothing is sent.

If you join a waitlist we ask only for your name and phone number or email, to contact you if a spot opens. In this demo that entry is saved in your own browser and nothing is sent.

If this site were live, we would collect only what we need to take a booking, use it for that purpose, keep it no longer than necessary and never sell it. You could ask to see or delete your information at any time by calling {{contact.phone}}.

Cookies and storage

This demo sets no tracking cookies. It uses your browser’s local storage only to remember small choices: your cookie choice, your language, a dismissed announcement, and any demo booking or waitlist entry you make. If analytics were added, they would run only after you accept.
